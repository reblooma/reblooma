About Reblooma

Massage is care.
The paperwork should say so.

Millions of people have money set aside for health care that could pay for the massage they already need. Almost none of it gets used that way, because no massage practice was set up to produce the documents. We started Reblooma to fix that part.

What we believe

Three ideas that shape every decision we make.

The setting isn't the test.

A day spa, a clinic, or someone who comes to your home. What qualifies care is the treatment and the documentation, not the room it happened in.

Practitioners shouldn't discount.

Patients pay less because their tax rate does the work. The practitioner's rate stays where they set it.

Paperwork should prove something.

A letter that nobody checks against the care proves nothing. The note and invoice show, session by session, that the treatment matched the letter.

Who does what

We're clear about what we do, and what we don't.

Healthcare paperwork only earns trust when every step has an accountable owner. Here is who owns each one.

Reblooma

Builds the software. Assembles your notes, invoices and letter into one file. Runs the practice tools.

Dr. B

An independent telehealth practice. Its clinicians review each request and decide whether to issue a letter.

Your practitioner

Does the treatment, then records what they worked on and signs the clinical note.

Your plan administrator

Decides whether a claim is reimbursed. Nobody else can promise that, including us.
