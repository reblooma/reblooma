Our mission

Access to therapeutic care shouldn't depend on who you know or what you can afford.

Massage therapy has a long and well-documented role in pain management, injury recovery, and chronic condition care. Yet for most Americans it remains out of reach — either unaffordable, uncovered, or simply unknown as a reimbursable option. Reblooma exists to change that. We built the clinical, billing, and marketplace infrastructure to make licensed massage therapy accessible to anyone with a genuine medical need — and to make it a sustainable, respected practice for the therapists who provide it.
