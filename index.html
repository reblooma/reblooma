FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need a doctor's referral to use Reblooma?
No referral needed. Reblooma's telehealth partner reviews your clinical intake and issues the Letter of Medical Necessity directly — no prior physician visit required.
What if my therapist already accepts FSA/HSA cards?
A card working at checkout doesn't mean the claim is audit-proof. Most providers lack clinical documentation — no CPT codes, no diagnosis, no SOAP note. Reblooma ensures your claim holds up if your FSA administrator ever requests documentation.
How does Reblooma work for practitioners who already have members?
You simply send your existing patients a Reblooma pre-checkin link before their appointment. They complete a 5-minute intake, receive their LMN, and can now pay with FSA/HSA. You don't need to change your booking system or workflow — just add Reblooma as a payment option.
How long does the LMN process take?
Most LMNs are issued same-day — typically within 2–4 hours of completing the pre-checkin intake. Your LMN covers all sessions for 12 months, so you only go through the process once per year.
Which FSA/HSA administrators does Reblooma support?
Your Support Package meets the documentation standards required by all major administrators — Wex, Optum Bank, HealthEquity, Fidelity, HSA Bank, and others. We also provide tailored submission guides for each administrator.
Is Reblooma only for massage therapy?
No — Reblooma supports massage therapy, infrared sauna, red light therapy, cold plunge, and other wellness modalities. If you're a wellness operator in a different category, reach out and we'll let you know our timeline for your modality.
