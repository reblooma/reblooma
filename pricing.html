How we're getting there

We'd rather get the price right than get it first.

  1. 1

    Run it with real practices

    A handful of testing practices use Reblooma with their own patients. We watch how long notes take, what gets submitted, and where it's clunky.

  2. 2

    Price against what people actually save

    The point is that FSA and HSA dollars make care cheaper. The price has to leave that obviously true, for patients and for practices.

  3. 3

    Publish it before launch

    The waitlist hears first. Nobody is charged by Reblooma before prices are public.

Help us set the price.

We're looking for a small number of massage practices to run Reblooma with real patients before launch. Tell us a little about your practice and we'll set up a short call.

Pricing questions

When will pricing be announced?+–

Before launch, once our testing practices have used Reblooma with real patients. Waitlist members hear first.

Does joining the waitlist cost anything?+–

No. Joining the waitlist is free and doesn't commit you to anything.

Is the letter consultation part of Reblooma's price?+–

No. Letters come from a consultation with Dr. B, an independent telehealth practice that sets and charges its own fee. Reblooma receives no part of it.2

What does a testing practice do?+–

Uses Reblooma with a few real patients, tells us honestly what works and what doesn't, and helps us land on prices that make sense. We'll talk it through on a short call before anything starts.
