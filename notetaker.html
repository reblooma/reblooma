Tips for accurate billing
  • Mention the total duration and time spent per technique
  • Name specific muscles and body regions treated
  • Include the patient's complaint (e.g. "chronic low back pain")
  • Note any adjunct modalities: heat, cold, stretching, dry needling
  • Document patient response and functional outcomes
  • Each 97124 / 97140 unit = 15 minutes of direct treatment time
