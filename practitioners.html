Questions therapists ask

1. What is an FSA or HSA? +–
Money set aside from a paycheck before taxes, for health care. Over 60 million Americans hold one. FSA funds expire yearly — nearly half forfeit some.
2. Does massage qualify? +–
More often than you'd think. Chronic shoulder and back pain, headaches, sleep problems, stress-related tension — you're almost certainly treating patients with these already. Relaxation alone doesn't qualify, but a documented condition does. The setting is never the test; the paperwork is.
3. I work at a day spa, a resort, or I'm mobile. Can I join? +–
Yes. Day spas, resorts, wellness practices and mobile or in-home therapists are all welcome — the setting was never the test. You need to be a licensed massage therapist, and the session has to treat what your client's letter covers. If your spa takes payment rather than you, we'll set things up with them so the invoice comes from the business that was paid.
4. I'm not clinically trained. Can I still write the notes? +–
Yes. You answer a few plain-language prompts — what your client told you, where you worked, what changed — and Reblooma turns them into a proper session note for you to check and sign. You never diagnose; the condition comes from the clinician's letter. If a session drifts outside what the letter covers, you'll see it before you sign.
5. My card machine already takes FSA/HSA cards. Do I need this? +–
A swipe clearing means the card was not declined, not that the visit qualifies. If the administrator later asks for proof and there is none, your patient repays it. That is the patient who quietly stops booking.
6. When do I get paid? +–
At the time of service. No waiting on an insurer or administrator, and your payout never depends on whether your patient is reimbursed.
7. What does it cost me? +–
The platform is free — no signup fee, nothing per session. One optional add-on: an AI seat that drafts your notes, priced before launch. Card processing fees apply to payouts, as with any terminal.
8. Who approves the claim? +–
Your patient's FSA/HSA plan administrator — not Reblooma, and not you. Nobody can promise reimbursement; Reblooma makes sure the documentation is ready when it is asked for.
